I've started work on Renewed Tab 2.0, a major update and partial rewrite of the
extension. This will bring improved performance and many new features, including
multiple widget dashboards, syncing between devices, and calendar/todo list
integrations. This update will also introduce Renewed Tab Plus.

<!-- more -->

Note: features may change between now and release.

## Improving the foundations

Renewed Tab was written when I was still learning React Native. It shows this,
with inconsistent performance and code that is hard to maintain. This update
includes a rewrite of the storage code, allowing for improved performance and
more capabilities. It also fixes many foundational issues with the UI.

## New features

### Multiple widget dashboards

Users will be able to create multiple dashboards of widgets. You could create a
personal dashboard and a work dashboard. Each dashboard can be individually configured with
backgrounds, themes, and other settings.

### Syncing between devices

You'll be able to sync your widgets between devices. Additionally, in a future
update, you'll be able to share dashboards with other users or your
organisation, creating shared dashboards for increased productivity.

### Calendar integrations

You'll be able to create a calendar widget and connect it to your Google,
Microsoft, or iCloud calendars. You'll be able to see upcoming events today and
in the week. Join video calls quickly from your new tab page.

### To-do list integrations

You'll be able to connect the to-do widget to to-do services including Todoist
and more to be confirmed.

### ...and more

After the 2.0 release, I plan to work on further features including preset
themes (including a light mode), improved background and customisation, and new
widgets.

## Plus membership

This update will introduce Renewed Tab Plus, a paid membership to support
development. This will be available as a cheap monthly or annual subscription.
Whilst unconfirmed, a lifetime purchase may be available.

A Plus membership will be required for multiple pages, sync, calendar, and to-do
integrations. Existing features will remain free. Renewed Tab will never contain
sell your data.

Renewed Tab is a side project by a solo developer. I am doing this because I
would like to start making commercial side projects and gain additional sources
of income. I believe that Renewed Tab has a lot of potential for development
here, and many things will be made possible that otherwise will not have
happened.

I am considering whether to make Renewed Tab closed source from 2.0. Renewed Tab
was created as a passion project and I never intended it to become a
collaborative open source project. I made the project open source because I
believed it was the right choice for user privacy and trust. With the advent of
LLMs scraping open source repos for capitalistic gain, I believe the
disadvantages for me outweight the gains.

## Conclusion

The update is in development and is expected to be launched early next year.
